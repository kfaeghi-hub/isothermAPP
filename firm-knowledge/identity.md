## How Isotherm runs a project

The sequence the documents describe, and the order the app models:

1. **Retention and Cx Plan** — scope, team, process and schedule fixed in writing;
   the plan is a living document, revised and reissued at milestones.
2. **Design review** — OPR and Basis of Design reviewed against the specification.
3. **Cx Index** — the matrix of systems, equipment and required verification.
4. **Installation verification** — IVC/PFC checklists per unit as work completes.
5. **Startup and prefunctional testing** — contractor-performed, Isotherm-witnessed.
6. **Functional performance testing** — Isotherm-directed, against the sequences.
7. **Issues Log** — every deficiency raised, tracked and closed with a date. The
   Issues Log is the backbone; site reports and meeting minutes reference it.
8. **Training and turnover** — O&M documentation, owner training, final report.

## Constants

- The **Issues Log is the backbone**. Anything that discovers a problem creates
  or links to an issue.
- **Completed records are frozen.** An issued document is never rewritten; a
  revision is a new document that says what changed.
- Appendices **reference the living records** rather than embedding a stale copy.
