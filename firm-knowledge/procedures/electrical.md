# Electrical distribution — procedure bullets

Selected by default when the project register carries `generator` or `ats`.

- Electrical equipment installation inspections (Isotherm)
- Torque verification and thermographic scan (by the contractors)
- Grounding and bonding verification (by the contractors)
- Emergency generator load bank testing (by the contractors, Isotherm witnessed)
- Automatic transfer switch operation and timing verification (Isotherm witnessed)
- Equipment initial startup checks (by the contractors or manufacturer/suppliers)
