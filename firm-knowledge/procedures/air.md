# Air handling and distribution — procedure bullets

Selected by default when the project register carries `ahu`, `fan`, `vav`,
`fcu` or `erv`.

- Equipment and ductwork installation inspections (Isotherm)
- Duct leakage testing (by the contractors) — per SMACNA and contract documents
- Filter installation and media verification (by the contractors)
- Fan rotation, belt tension and vibration verification (by the contractors)
- Equipment initial startup checks (by the contractors or manufacturer/suppliers)
- Damper stroke and end-switch verification (Isotherm witnessed)
- Terminal unit airflow verification (by the balancing contractor)
- Operational testing under various loads (prefunctional testing/verification by the mechanical contractor)
