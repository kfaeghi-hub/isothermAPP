# Hydronic piping and pumps — procedure bullets

Selected by default when the project register carries `pump`, `boiler`,
`chiller` or `cooling_tower`. Toggleable per project in the wizard.

- Piping and equipment installation inspections (Isotherm)
- Pipe flushing and cleaning (by the contractors) — requirements per mechanical specification and contract documents
- Pipe pressure testing (by the contractors) — criteria per mechanical specification and contract documents
- Chemical treatment and water/glycol analysis where applicable (by the contractors)
- Equipment initial startup checks (by the contractors or manufacturer/suppliers)
- Pump rotation, alignment and vibration verification (by the contractors)
- Operational testing under various loads (prefunctional testing/verification by the mechanical contractor)
- Expansion tank charge and air separator verification (Isotherm witnessed)
